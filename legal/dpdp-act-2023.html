01About the Act

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”) is the law that governs how personal data in digital form is processed in India. It sets out what organisations must do when they collect and use your data, and what rights you have over it. The full text is published by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY): read the official Act (PDF).

This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. If anything here differs from the Act or the rules made under it, the Act and the rules prevail.

02Key terms

Data Principal

You

The person the data is about. For a child, this includes their parent or lawful guardian.

Data Fiduciary

Gracenotes

The organisation that decides why and how your data is used.

Data Processor

Our providers

A service that handles data on our behalf, such as our form-delivery service.

Child

Under 18

Anyone below 18 years of age. Extra protections apply to their data.

04Children’s data (Section 9)

Because many Gracenotes learners are under 18, this part of the Act matters most to us. Before processing a child’s personal data, the Act requires verifiable consent of the child’s parent or lawful guardian. It also prohibits processing that is likely to harm a child’s well-being, and prohibits tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children.

If you are a parent or guardian, you are the person who gives consent for your child’s data and who exercises your child’s rights.

06Your duties as a Data Principal

The Act also asks you to: follow the law, avoid impersonating another person or suppressing material information when giving us your data, not file false or frivolous complaints, and give verifiably authentic information when exercising your rights (Section 15).

07How to exercise your rights

  1. Email info@gracenotes-music.com from the address you used to contact us, saying which right you want to use.
  2. We may ask you to confirm your identity (and, for a child, that you are their parent or guardian).
  3. We will respond within the time prescribed by law.
  4. If you are not satisfied, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India after using our grievance process.
