01Who we are

This website is run by Gracenotes School of Music, operated by Tempogrid Pvt Ltd (“Gracenotes”, “we”, “us”). For the purposes of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”), we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described in this policy.

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@gracenotes-music.com or +91 91238 26035.

We do not ask for payment details, government ID numbers or other sensitive information through this website, and we ask you not to send them in enquiries. We do not currently run analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

03Why we use your data

We use your data only for the purpose you gave it to us:

  • to reply to your enquiry and answer your questions about classes, class formats and getting started;
  • to arrange a conversation, assessment or demonstration if you ask for one;
  • to keep a record of the enquiry so that we can follow up with you; and
  • to meet legal obligations and to protect our rights where the law requires or allows us to.

Our lawful basis is your consent. When you tick the consent box on the enquiry form, you agree to us using your details for the purposes above. You can withdraw that consent at any time (see Your rights). We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

05Transfers outside India

Some of the service providers above may process data on servers located outside India. The DPDP Act permits this except to countries the Central Government has restricted by notification. We take reasonable steps to choose providers that apply appropriate security safeguards.

06How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only for as long as it is needed to respond to you and follow up on your enquiry. If you do not go on to enrol, we delete or anonymise it once that purpose is complete, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. If you enrol, we will give you a separate notice explaining what we collect for teaching and administration and how long we keep it.

07Children’s data

Many of our learners are under 18. Under the DPDP Act, a “child” is a person under 18 years of age, and a Data Fiduciary must obtain verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child’s personal data.

  • Enquiries about a child must be made by the child’s parent or legal guardian. The consent box on our enquiry form confirms this.
  • We will not track or behaviourally monitor children, and we will not direct targeted advertising at them.
  • If you believe a child has sent us personal data without a parent or guardian’s consent, please tell us at info@gracenotes-music.com and we will delete it.

08Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you (or, for a child, the parent or guardian) have the right to:

Access

Know what we hold

Ask for a summary of the personal data we process and the processing activities.

Correct & erase

Fix or delete it

Ask us to correct, complete or update your data, or to erase it when it is no longer needed.

Withdraw

Take back consent

Withdraw consent at any time. It is as easy to withdraw as it was to give.

Grievance

Raise a complaint

Use our grievance contact below. You can approach the Data Protection Board of India if it is not resolved.

Nominate

Name someone

Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, email info@gracenotes-music.com from the address you used to contact us, and tell us what you would like us to do. We may need to verify your identity first. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place, and we may not be able to answer an enquiry we no longer hold details for.

09How we protect your data

We use reasonable security safeguards to protect personal data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please share only what you are comfortable sending.
