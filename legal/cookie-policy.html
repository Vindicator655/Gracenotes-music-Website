01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website or a service it uses can store on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, do the same job. They are used to make sites work, to remember preferences, and sometimes to measure or track visitors.

02What this website does

At the time of writing, the Gracenotes website does not set its own cookies, and does not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies or pixels. Because of that, we do not show a cookie banner.

The site does not use browser storage (such as local storage) to track you either.

03Third-party services that load on the site

Our pages load a few resources from other providers. When your browser fetches them, the provider receives technical data such as your IP address and browser details — this is how the internet works, and it can happen even without cookies. We use these services only to display the site and handle your enquiry, not for tracking or advertising.

ServiceWhat it does here
Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)Delivers the Montserrat and Source Sans fonts.
Unsplash image delivery (images.unsplash.com)Delivers the background image at the top of the home page.
Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com)Receives your enquiry when you press “Send Enquiry”. See the Privacy Policy.

If you follow a link to Instagram or WhatsApp, you leave our site and those services’ own cookie and privacy policies apply.

04If this changes

If we later add analytics, embedded media or anything else that uses non-essential cookies, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent through a cookie banner before those cookies are set.

05How you can control cookies

  • Browser settings: all major browsers let you block or delete cookies and clear site data. Check the “Privacy” or “Security” section of your browser’s settings.
  • Private browsing: private or incognito windows discard cookies when you close them.
  • Blocking third-party requests: browser extensions can stop third-party resources such as fonts from loading. The site will still work, but may look different.

06Questions

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at info@gracenotes-music.com. For how we handle personal data generally, see our Privacy Policy.
